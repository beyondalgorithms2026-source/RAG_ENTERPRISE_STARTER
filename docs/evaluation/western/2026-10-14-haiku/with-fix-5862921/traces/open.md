# WQ-10 (open)

**Question:** A customer asks Support to promise a refund on the call. What should the agent do?

**Answer:** The agent must not promise a refund on the call. Support may acknowledge the request, but may not approve a refund unless a written delegation exists [S2]. The playbook states that Support must not promise a refund and that agents may acknowledge a refund request and route it for approval [S4]. Finance approves billing credits and refunds unless a contract expressly delegates a small service credit to another role [S6]. The agent should use the approved acknowledgement language: the request has been recorded and routed to the team that reviews commercial adjustments, the agent cannot confirm an amount or outcome from Support, and the customer will be updated after the review [S2]. The agent should avoid phrases such as "refund guaranteed," "we owe you," or "automatic credit" unless signed customer terms clearly require that outcome and the commercial owner has confirmed it [S2]. If the issue is a billing correction caused by a clear administrative error, route it to Finance; if it is a service-credit request tied to SLA performance, route it to Customer Success and Finance with the relevant ticket and incident timestamps [S2]. Any call recording needed for a complaint, incident or contractual dispute may be placed on hold before automatic deletion [S1].
