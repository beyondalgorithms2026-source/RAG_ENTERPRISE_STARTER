# WQ-10 (open)

**Question:** A customer asks Support to promise a refund on the call. What should the agent do?

**Answer:** The agent should not promise a refund on the call. Support must not promise a refund, and agents may acknowledge the request and route it for approval [S4]. Support may acknowledge a refund or service-credit request but may not approve one unless a written delegation exists [S2]. Finance approves billing credits and refunds unless a contract expressly delegates a small service credit to another role [S6]. The agent should use the approved wording: "I understand why you're asking for a credit. I've recorded the request and routed it to the team that reviews commercial adjustments. I can't confirm an amount or outcome from Support, but we'll update you after the review" [S2]. The agent should avoid phrases such as "refund guaranteed," "we owe you," or "automatic credit" unless signed customer terms clearly require that outcome and the commercial owner has confirmed it [S2]. Routing depends on the cause: a billing correction caused by a clear administrative error goes to Finance, and a service-credit request tied to SLA performance goes to Customer Success and Finance with the relevant ticket and incident timestamps [S2]. If the call is needed for a complaint, incident or contractual dispute, it may be placed on hold before automatic deletion, which is a retention matter rather than a refund decision [S1].
