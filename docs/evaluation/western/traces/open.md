# WQ-10 (open)

**Question:** A customer asks Support to promise a refund on the call. What should the agent do?

**Answer:** The agent should acknowledge the refund request but may not promise a refund. Instead, they should inform the customer that the request has been recorded and routed to the team that reviews commercial adjustments. The agent cannot confirm an amount or outcome from Support, and should avoid using terms like 'refund guaranteed' unless explicitly required by signed customer terms and confirmed by the commercial owner [S2][S4].
