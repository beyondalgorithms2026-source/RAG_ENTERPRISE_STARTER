# T3 — the five facts (canonical wording, 14 Oct 2026)

Every public place (live pages, the three READMEs, the scorecard, the Loom script, Upwork, LinkedIn, the
spanfold.com draft) states these with the same values. Drafts in steps 6–8 copy this wording.

1. **Answer model.** Answers are generated by Claude Haiku 5.5 (since 12 Oct 2026). Embeddings use OpenAI
   text-embedding-3-small (384 dimensions).
2. **Corpus.** All documents are synthetic, for two fictional companies: Northline Analytics (US/EU policies) and
   Northwind Logistics (the original demo).
3. **Scorecard.** Northline demo set: 19 of 20 on Claude Haiku 5.5 (14 Oct 2026). The one miss, WQ-19, is blocked by
   input screening; the planted instruction is not obeyed. Earlier: 15 of 20 on gpt-4o-mini (5 Oct 2026).
4. **Red team.** 20 scenarios, 20 defended: 18 deterministic checks plus 2 live access-control checks in CI
   (release 17 Sep 2026; live checks re-passed 14 Oct 2026).
5. **Limitations.** Synthetic data; a 20-question demo set run once, not a benchmark; free hosting with cold starts;
   not a production or client deployment. Northwind results (25/25 baseline, 82/90 candidate) were produced on
   gpt-4o-mini.
