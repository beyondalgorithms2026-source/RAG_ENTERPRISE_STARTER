Remote Work Guidelines for Engineering

Based on the 2024 Employee Handbook and the recent Engineering Charter update, remote work is classified under a Hybrid-First Model. Engineers are required to be in-office at least 2 days per week, typically Tuesday and Wednesday, for core synchronization meetings [1].

For senior-level engineers (L5+), fully remote exceptions can be granted by the VP of Engineering, provided there is a documented history of high performance and a 4-hour timezone overlap with the headquarters' primary zone [2]. Stipends for home office setups are capped at $1,500 annually for all full-time engineering staff [3].
