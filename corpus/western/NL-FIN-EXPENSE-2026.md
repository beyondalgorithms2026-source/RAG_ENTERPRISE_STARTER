# Travel and Expense Policy

## Executive summary

Northline reimburses reasonable business expenses that are necessary, supported by records and approved at the right level. Employees should spend company money as carefully as they would spend their own.

The standard daily meal cap is **£65 in the UK**, **$85 in the US** and **€75 in the Netherlands/EU**. Standard hotel caps are city-specific and are listed in `NL-FIN-EXPENSE-APPENDIX-RATES`; they are **£240 for London**, **€220 for Amsterdam**, **$220 for Austin** and **$300 for New York**, excluding mandatory taxes and fees. Receipts are required for individual expenses of **£25 / $30 / €30 or more** and for all hotels, airfare, rail, rental cars and client entertainment regardless of amount.

Routine in-policy claims need the employee's manager approval. Over-cap or policy-exception items need **manager plus Finance approval**. A single planned purchase or travel commitment above **£1,000 / $1,250 / €1,150** needs Finance pre-approval before booking. A trip expected to exceed **£2,500 / $3,200 / €2,900** needs functional-director plus Finance pre-approval.

## Scope

This policy applies to employees and directors incurring costs on behalf of Northline Analytics Ltd and its UK, US and Netherlands operations. Contractors may claim expenses only where their contract permits it and must follow the same evidence rules unless the contract states otherwise.

This policy covers travel, accommodation, meals, local transport, client entertainment, small business purchases and other reimbursable out-of-pocket costs. It does not govern procurement contracts, recurring software subscriptions or supplier onboarding; those are covered by `NL-PROC-VENDOR-2026`.

## General rules

A reimbursable expense must be:

- for a genuine Northline business purpose;
- reasonable in amount and consistent with this policy;
- supported by a receipt or equivalent record when required;
- coded to the correct department, customer or project;
- submitted within **30 calendar days** of the expense or trip end;
- approved by someone who did not personally incur the expense.

Employees must not split a purchase into smaller transactions to avoid an approval threshold.

Expense reimbursements are not a substitute for salary, bonuses or personal allowances. Personal costs must not be placed on a company card except for an unavoidable mixed charge that is immediately identified and repaid.

## Approval matrix

| Situation | Required approval |
|---|---|
| In-policy expense within caps | Line manager |
| Over-cap meal, hotel or transport exception | Line manager + Finance |
| Single planned purchase/commitment over £1,000 / $1,250 / €1,150 | Finance pre-approval + line manager |
| Trip expected over £2,500 / $3,200 / €2,900 | Functional director + Finance pre-approval |
| Client entertainment over £300 / $375 / €350 total | Functional director + Finance |
| Expense submitted more than 30 days late | Line manager + Finance |
| Missing required receipt | Line manager + Finance with written explanation |
| Expense for the CEO | Board-designated approver or CFO-equivalent role |

Finance may ask for additional evidence before reimbursement even where the manager has approved the claim.

## Travel booking

Employees should use the company travel portal where available. Economy class is the default for air and rail travel.

Premium economy may be approved for a scheduled flight longer than **6 hours** where the fare is no more than **£350 / $450 / €400** above the lowest reasonable economy fare, or where a medical accommodation has been approved. Business class requires the CFO or CEO and is normally limited to exceptional circumstances such as a scheduled flight longer than **9 hours** combined with an immediate critical customer or board commitment.

Travelers should select sensible schedules rather than the absolute cheapest itinerary when the cheapest option creates an unreasonable connection, unsafe arrival time or more than two additional hours of travel.

Unused ticket credits belong to Northline and should be reused for later business travel where practical.

## Hotels

The hotel caps in `NL-FIN-EXPENSE-APPENDIX-RATES` are nightly room-rate caps, exclusive of mandatory occupancy taxes and unavoidable booking fees.

City caps are:

| City | Nightly cap |
|---|---:|
| London | £240 |
| Amsterdam | €220 |
| Austin | $220 |
| New York | $300 |

For other cities, the default cap is **£180 / $220 / €200 per night**. Where a conference hotel, customer-mandated venue, safety issue or exceptional local demand makes the cap impractical, the employee should obtain manager and Finance approval before booking where possible.

Minibar, in-room entertainment and hotel spa charges are personal and not reimbursable. Hotel internet is reimbursable where not included in the room rate and needed for work.

## Meals and per diem

Northline normally reimburses actual meal costs up to the daily cap rather than paying a flat cash allowance. The daily caps are:

- UK: **£65 per day**;
- US: **$85 per day**;
- Netherlands/EU: **€75 per day**.

The cap covers breakfast, lunch, dinner, non-alcoholic drinks, reasonable tips where customary and small food-delivery fees. Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of approved client entertainment or a company-hosted team dinner and should be moderate.

When breakfast is included with the hotel or an event provides a meal, employees should not claim a second full meal merely because the daily cap has not been reached.

Finance may approve a fixed per diem for a specific trip. If so, the approved rate in the trip authorisation replaces actual-meal reimbursement for the covered days; employees must not claim both.

## Ground transport and mileage

Employees should choose safe, practical transport. Public transport is preferred when it is reasonable for the route and luggage. Taxi or ride-hail use is reimbursable for airport transfers, late-night travel, accessibility needs, carrying equipment, or where it saves material working time.

Personal-vehicle mileage is reimbursed at the company rates in the rates appendix:

- UK: **£0.45 per business mile for the first 10,000 business miles in a holiday year; £0.25 thereafter**;
- US: **$0.67 per business mile**;
- Netherlands/EU: **€0.30 per business kilometre**.

These are Northline reimbursement rates for this corpus and should not be read as statements of statutory tax treatment.

Normal commuting between home and the employee's contractual office is not reimbursable. Travel from the contractual office to a customer site is business travel. For home-based employees, Finance may apply the same principle by treating ordinary travel to the designated local hub as commuting where the employment terms say so.

## Client entertainment

Client entertainment must have a clear business purpose and a named Northline host. The claim must record the customer or prospect organisation, attendee count and business purpose.

Client entertainment up to **£300 / $375 / €350 total per event** requires manager approval if otherwise reasonable. Above that amount, functional-director and Finance approval is required.

Cash gifts, gift cards and entertainment that could reasonably be viewed as an improper inducement are not reimbursable. See `NL-HR-CODE-2026` for conflicts and anti-bribery expectations.

## Home-office and equipment expenses

Standard laptops, monitors, security keys and other core equipment are purchased by IT rather than through personal expense claims. Small pre-approved accessories may be expensed up to **£100 / $125 / €115 per item** with manager approval. Above that amount, IT or Finance should purchase the item directly.

The remote-work equipment allowance is governed by `NL-OPS-REMOTE-2026`: up to **£600 / $750 / €700 once every 36 months** for approved home-office equipment. Claims against that allowance still require receipts and may not duplicate equipment already supplied by Northline.

## Receipts and evidence

A receipt is required for:

- every hotel, flight, rail, rental-car and client-entertainment claim;
- any individual expense of **£25 / $30 / €30 or more**;
- any transaction where Finance specifically requests evidence.

For smaller routine items below the threshold, the employee must still enter the supplier, date, amount, currency and business purpose.

A card slip showing only a total is not a sufficient receipt for client entertainment if an itemised receipt is reasonably available. Digital receipts are acceptable.

If a required receipt is lost, the employee must provide a written explanation and whatever alternative evidence exists, such as a booking confirmation or card record. Missing-receipt claims require manager and Finance approval.

## Currency conversion

Claims should be submitted in the currency of the transaction where the expense system supports it. The system's posted card rate or approved accounting exchange rate will be used for reimbursement. Employees should not add their own foreign-exchange mark-up.

Where a personal card charges a separately identifiable foreign-transaction fee on a valid business expense, that fee is reimbursable.

## Company cards

A company card is for business use only. Cardholders remain responsible for attaching receipts and coding transactions by the monthly close deadline.

A suspected lost, stolen or compromised company card must be reported to the card issuer and Finance immediately. Fraudulent or unknown transactions should not be coded as ordinary expenses.

Repeated failure to provide receipts, personal use of the card or deliberate circumvention of limits may result in card suspension and action under the code of conduct.

## Non-reimbursable expenses

Northline does not normally reimburse personal clothing, leisure upgrades, traffic or parking fines, personal childcare, pet care, minibar charges, personal entertainment, tobacco or nicotine products, personal travel insurance, loyalty-program purchases, or costs incurred by a companion who is not travelling for Northline business.

Reasonable laundry is reimbursable on a business trip of **7 consecutive nights or more**.

Personal passport costs are not reimbursable. A visa or entry authorisation required solely for approved Northline business travel may be reimbursed with the travel approval.

## Timing and payment

Employees should submit a completed claim within 30 calendar days. Managers should approve or return it within 5 working days. Finance aims to include approved claims in the next scheduled reimbursement run.

Claims older than 90 days may be rejected unless there is a documented reason for delay. Finance and the employee's manager must approve any late exception.

## Exceptions

An exception should be requested before the cost is incurred wherever practical. The request should state the business reason, expected amount and why a compliant alternative is not reasonable.

Emergency safety decisions take priority over a cap. For example, an employee should use a safe hotel or transport option during a disruption rather than remain in an unsafe situation to stay within policy. The employee should document the circumstances afterwards.

## Appendix relationship

`NL-FIN-EXPENSE-APPENDIX-RATES` is an operative appendix to this policy. Where a rate in the appendix conflicts with this policy, the newer effective document listed in `NL-POLICY-CHANGELOG-2026` controls. Finance must update both documents when changing a shared cap.

## Ownership and review

Policy owner: Finance. Travel operations and expense administration: Finance Operations. Managers are responsible for first-line review. This policy supersedes `NL-FIN-EXPENSE-2025` from **1 April 2026**.
