# Expense Rates Appendix

## Purpose

This appendix is part of `NL-FIN-EXPENSE-2026`. It contains the numeric reimbursement rates most likely to change independently of the main policy. All amounts are synthetic Northline operating limits.

## Meals

| Region | Daily meal cap |
|---|---:|
| United Kingdom | £65 |
| United States | $85 |
| Netherlands/EU | €75 |

The meal cap covers ordinary meals, non-alcoholic drinks, reasonable tips where customary and small delivery fees. It is not a cash entitlement.

## Hotel caps

| City | Nightly room-rate cap |
|---|---:|
| London | £240 |
| Amsterdam | €220 |
| Austin | $220 |
| New York | $300 |
| Other UK city | £180 |
| Other US city | $220 |
| Other NL/EU city | €200 |

Mandatory occupancy taxes and unavoidable booking fees are outside the room-rate cap.

## Mileage

| Location | Northline reimbursement rate |
|---|---:|
| UK, first 10,000 business miles in holiday year | £0.45 per mile |
| UK, above 10,000 business miles | £0.25 per mile |
| US | $0.67 per mile |
| Netherlands/EU | €0.30 per kilometre |

These are company reimbursement rates for this corpus, not tax advice.

## Approval thresholds

## Home-office allowance

The remote-work allowance is **£600 / $750 / €700 once every 36 months** for approved equipment, as described in `NL-OPS-REMOTE-2026`.
