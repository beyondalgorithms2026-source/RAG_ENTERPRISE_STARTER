# Leave, Sickness and Parental Leave Policy

## Executive summary

Northline Analytics Ltd operates a common leave year from **1 January to 31 December**. UK employees receive **25 days of company annual leave plus 8 bank holidays** for the England and Wales calendar used by the Manchester office. Netherlands employees receive **25 days of company annual leave plus local public holidays**. Austin employees receive **20 days of paid time off (PTO) plus 10 company holidays**. Part-time entitlements are pro-rated.

This policy also sets Northline's company sick-pay and parental-pay arrangements. UK statutory rights continue to apply where they are more favourable or create a separate entitlement. Northline's enhanced payments are company benefits; they do not reduce statutory leave rights. This document is an operating policy, not a legal opinion.

## Scope and principles

This policy applies to employees of Northline Analytics Ltd and its controlled UK, US and Netherlands operations. It does not apply to independent contractors except where a written contractor agreement expressly adopts a specific provision.

The policy is intended to make routine leave decisions predictable. Managers should approve reasonable requests where team coverage permits and should not discourage staff from using earned leave. HR owns interpretation of this policy. Where local law provides a greater entitlement than this document, the local entitlement prevails.

Northline uses the following working assumptions for this synthetic corpus:

- the company holiday year starts on 1 January and ends on 31 December;
- the Manchester office follows the England and Wales bank-holiday calendar;
- the Austin office follows the Northline US company-holiday calendar;
- the Amsterdam office follows the Northline NL public-holiday calendar;
- one standard working day means the employee's normal contracted day.

For UK workers, the company allowance of 25 days plus 8 bank holidays is deliberately above the common statutory floor for a five-day worker. GOV.UK states that most workers working five days a week are entitled to at least 28 days' paid annual leave and that bank holidays may be included within statutory leave.

## Annual leave in the UK

### Entitlement

A full-time Manchester employee receives **25 days of paid annual leave** in each full holiday year, plus **8 bank holidays**. The annual leave allowance excludes bank holidays; the two figures are separate.

Part-time employees receive 25 days pro-rated to their contracted working pattern, plus bank-holiday treatment pro-rated where necessary to avoid part-time employees being disadvantaged by the days on which bank holidays fall.

Employees joining or leaving during a holiday year receive a pro-rated allowance calculated by completed calendar days of employment. HR rounds the final balance to the nearest half-day where the HR system cannot record smaller units.

### Booking leave

Employees should submit normal annual-leave requests in the HR system before making non-refundable personal travel arrangements. The expected notice is:

| Leave requested | Normal notice expected |
|---|---:|
| 1–2 working days | at least 5 working days |
| 3–5 working days | at least 10 working days |
| More than 5 working days | at least 20 working days |

These are planning rules, not absolute barriers. Managers may approve shorter notice for family events, urgent personal matters or where team coverage is straightforward.

A manager may decline or propose alternative dates where granting the request would leave customer support, security response, payroll, month-end finance or a committed delivery milestone materially uncovered. A refusal should state the operational reason and should not be used to prevent an employee taking their annual entitlement across the year.

### Carry-over

Employees should normally take annual leave in the year in which it is earned. Up to **5 unused company annual-leave days** may be carried into the next holiday year with manager approval. Carried days must normally be used by **31 March** or they expire, subject to statutory rules that require additional carry-over, including circumstances connected with certain family leave or sickness.

Managers must not tell an employee to forfeit statutory leave where the law requires it to be preserved. HR should be consulted for long sickness absence, family leave crossing year-end, or any case in which leave could not reasonably be taken because of company requirements.

### Buying or selling leave

Northline does not operate a general annual buy-or-sell scheme in 2026. A local contract may contain a legacy arrangement; HR must confirm it before Payroll acts.

## Sickness absence in the UK

### Reporting sickness

An employee who is too unwell to work should notify their manager or the designated team channel **by 10:00 local time** on the first day of absence, or as soon as reasonably possible if they are receiving urgent medical care. The employee should say that they are unwell, give an expected duration if known, and identify urgent handovers. They do not need to disclose detailed diagnosis information to the team.

For sickness lasting more than seven calendar days, HR may request medical evidence consistent with current UK requirements and company practice. Sensitive medical information is Confidential and should be stored only in the HR system or another HR-approved location.

### Company sick pay

For UK employees who have completed probation, Northline provides up to **20 working days of company sick pay at 100% of base salary in a rolling 12-month period**. During probation, Northline provides up to **5 working days at 100% of base salary** in a rolling 12-month period.

After the company-paid allowance is exhausted, eligible employees move to the statutory sick-pay arrangement then in force, subject to the applicable eligibility and duration rules. The statutory amount may change, so this policy intentionally does not hard-code a monetary rate. Statutory sick pay can coexist with a more generous company sick-pay scheme.

Company sick pay covers ordinary short-term illness, recovery from treatment, and medically necessary absence. It does not convert annual leave into sick leave automatically: if an employee becomes ill while on holiday, they should contact HR promptly and provide evidence if requested.

### Longer-term absence and return to work

After 10 consecutive working days of sickness, HR should agree a contact plan with the employee. The plan should be proportionate and should avoid repeated requests for the same medical information. Depending on circumstances, Northline may discuss a phased return, temporary duty changes, workstation changes or other reasonable workplace adjustments.

A return-to-work conversation is expected after any sickness absence of 5 or more consecutive working days, and may also be used after repeated shorter absences. The purpose is to confirm fitness to return, identify work-related causes or support needs, and correct the attendance record. It is not automatically a disciplinary meeting.

## UK maternity leave and Northline top-up

### Statutory framework

Northline administers Statutory Maternity Leave in line with the rules in force when leave is taken. As of the research basis used for this synthetic 2026 corpus, Statutory Maternity Leave is **52 weeks**, made up of 26 weeks of Ordinary Maternity Leave and 26 weeks of Additional Maternity Leave.

The employee does not lose annual-leave accrual because they are on maternity leave. HR will confirm statutory notice, evidence and pay calculations based on the employee's circumstances and the rules in force at the time.

### Northline enhanced maternity pay

An employee with at least **26 weeks of continuous Northline service by the end of the 15th week before the expected week of childbirth** receives the following Northline enhanced maternity package, provided they meet the administrative requirements for maternity leave:

| Period | Northline payment |
|---|---|
| Weeks 1–16 | 100% of normal base salary, inclusive of any statutory maternity pay due |
| Weeks 17–26 | 50% of normal base salary, or the statutory amount if greater, inclusive of statutory maternity pay |
| Remaining statutory paid period | Statutory maternity pay, where eligible |
| Remaining leave to week 52 | Unpaid, unless another statutory or company entitlement applies |

Employees with less than the service threshold retain their statutory rights and receive statutory pay where eligible, but do not receive the enhanced top-up unless HR confirms an exception in writing.

“Normal base salary” excludes discretionary bonus, sales commission, overtime, one-off allowances and expense reimbursement. Pension and benefit treatment follows the relevant scheme rules and statutory requirements.

There is no requirement to repay enhanced maternity pay solely because an employee decides not to return after leave, unless a separate written agreement expressly applies to a distinct benefit.

## UK paternity leave and partner leave

Northline follows statutory paternity-leave rules in force at the time of the birth or placement. The current framework used for this corpus provides up to **2 weeks of paternity leave**, which may be taken together or as separate one-week blocks, with timing rules around the child's birth or placement.

For an eligible UK employee, Northline pays **100% of normal base salary for up to 2 weeks of paternity leave**, inclusive of any statutory paternity pay due.

Northline also permits up to **2 paid working days** for antenatal or adoption-related appointments for an employee supporting a partner, where those days are not already paid under a statutory right or another company benefit. This is a company benefit and does not reduce statutory paternity leave.

## Adoption leave

Northline provides an enhanced adoption package on substantially the same company-pay basis as enhanced maternity pay for the employee taking the primary adoption-leave entitlement:

- weeks 1–16 at 100% of normal base salary, inclusive of statutory adoption pay where due;
- weeks 17–26 at 50% of normal base salary, or the statutory amount if greater;
- the rest of the statutory paid period at the statutory rate where eligible;
- the remaining statutory leave period unpaid unless another entitlement applies.

The same 26-week Northline-service threshold described for enhanced maternity pay applies to the company top-up, without reducing any statutory right.

## Shared Parental Leave

Where an employee qualifies for Shared Parental Leave (SPL), Northline will process the statutory entitlement in force at the time. Under the current framework used for this corpus, eligible parents may share up to **50 weeks of leave and up to 37 weeks of pay** after curtailing maternity or adoption leave and pay as required.

Northline enhances the first **6 weeks of SPL actually taken by a Northline employee to 100% of normal base salary**, inclusive of statutory shared-parental pay, provided the employee has at least 26 weeks of continuous Northline service at the relevant eligibility point. Enhanced SPL is capped at 6 weeks per Northline employee per birth or placement, even if leave is taken in several blocks.

The SPL enhancement does not stack with maternity or adoption enhancement for the same employee beyond the applicable company maximum. HR will confirm the interaction before leave begins.

## Neonatal Care Leave

Where a newborn requires qualifying neonatal care, Northline will administer statutory Neonatal Care Leave and Pay in force at the time. The current framework used for this corpus provides one week of leave for every seven full and continuous days in neonatal care, up to **12 weeks**, in addition to other parental leave.

Northline enhances eligible Neonatal Care Leave to **100% of normal base salary for up to 4 weeks**, inclusive of statutory neonatal-care pay. Any remaining qualifying leave is paid at the statutory rate where the employee is eligible.

## Parental bereavement and compassionate leave

An employee dealing with the death of a child, partner or close family member should contact HR or their manager when able. The manager should prioritise practical support over paperwork during the first days.

Northline provides **5 paid working days of compassionate leave** for the death of a spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, parent-in-law or another person for whom the employee has primary caring responsibility. HR may approve up to a further 5 paid days in exceptional circumstances, including overseas funeral travel or where the employee is arranging the funeral.

Eligible UK parents also retain statutory Parental Bereavement Leave and Pay. The statutory scheme may apply where a child dies before age 18 or following a stillbirth after 24 weeks of pregnancy. Northline tops up up to **2 weeks of qualifying statutory parental-bereavement leave to 100% of normal base salary**.

Compassionate leave does not reduce the statutory bereavement entitlement.

## Emergency time off and caring responsibilities

Employees may take reasonable time off to deal with an emergency involving a dependant in accordance with local law. Managers should distinguish an immediate emergency from longer-term caring arrangements. Where a longer absence is needed, HR may discuss annual leave, unpaid leave, flexible working, parental leave or another applicable statutory entitlement.

Northline also provides **3 paid emergency-care days per holiday year** for an unexpected breakdown in care arrangements affecting a dependant. These days are not carried over and are not paid out on termination.

## Austin office PTO

### Entitlement

Regular full-time employees assigned to the Austin office receive **20 days of PTO per holiday year**, plus **10 Northline US company holidays**. PTO is a combined bank for vacation, personal days and ordinary short-term illness. It is not separated into vacation and sick buckets in this synthetic policy.

New starters receive a pro-rated PTO balance for the remainder of the calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated allowance. Employees should use the same booking notice standards used elsewhere in this policy where practical.

Northline's US PTO is a company benefit rather than a statement of a federal vacation entitlement.

### Carry-over and negative balances

Austin employees may carry over up to **5 unused PTO days** into the next holiday year. Carried days should normally be used by 31 March unless local law or written employment terms require different treatment. Negative PTO balances are not normally permitted.

### Extended medical or family leave

An Austin employee needing an extended medical, parental, military, disability-related or other protected leave should contact People Operations. Northline will assess applicable federal, Texas and other protected-leave requirements based on the employee's circumstances and worksite eligibility. This policy does not attempt to restate every US protected-leave rule.

Northline provides **12 weeks of company parental leave at 100% of base salary** to regular Austin employees with at least 6 months of service, for birth, adoption or placement of a child. The 12 weeks may run concurrently with any legally protected leave where permitted. Employees with less than 6 months' service may request up to 6 weeks of unpaid company parental leave, subject to any greater legal entitlement.

## Amsterdam office annual leave and family leave

Regular full-time employees assigned to Amsterdam receive **25 days of paid annual leave per holiday year**, plus the local public holidays designated on Northline's NL holiday calendar. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated annual-leave allowance.

The Amsterdam office follows mandatory Dutch statutory leave and family-leave rights where those exceed or differ from this group policy. People Operations in the EU office should confirm the local entitlement before approving a case that relies on statutory maternity, partner, parental, care or sickness rules. This document intentionally does not attempt to reproduce Dutch statutory pay formulas.

## Unpaid leave and sabbaticals

An employee with at least 12 months of service may request unpaid personal leave of up to **20 working days**. Approval depends on business coverage, performance standing, the purpose and timing of the request, and any legal entitlement. Requests longer than 20 working days require the relevant functional director and Head of People approval.

Northline does not operate an automatic paid sabbatical programme in 2026.

## Public holidays during leave

A UK bank holiday that falls during maternity, adoption or another form of statutory family leave does not replace a day of statutory leave. Annual-leave accrual continues as required. HR will normally arrange for accrued annual leave to be taken before or after family leave by agreement.

For ordinary annual leave, a UK bank holiday is not deducted from the employee's 25-day annual-leave balance. For Austin, a Northline US company holiday is not deducted from the 20-day PTO balance if it falls on a day the employee would otherwise have worked.

## Recording and approvals

Employees must record annual leave, PTO and planned family leave in the HR system. Managers approve annual leave and ordinary PTO. HR approves the classification and pay treatment of maternity, adoption, paternity, shared-parental, neonatal, bereavement, extended sickness and other protected leave.

A manager must not approve a special pay arrangement outside this policy without written HR and Finance approval.

## Privacy and records

Leave records are Internal unless they contain health, family or other sensitive personal information, in which case they are Confidential. Medical certificates, pregnancy information and detailed family circumstances must not be copied into general team folders, Jira tickets or customer systems.

Retention of HR and leave records follows `NL-SEC-DATA-RETENTION-2026`.

## Manager checklist

Before approving or declining leave, a manager should confirm the employee's balance, team coverage, critical customer commitments and whether the request may involve a statutory or protected leave category. When in doubt, the manager should pause the decision and ask HR rather than improvise a rule.

For family leave, managers should avoid promising a pay figure. HR will issue the written leave and pay confirmation after eligibility is checked.

## Practical leave examples

### UK annual leave around bank holidays

A Manchester employee who takes Monday to Friday off in a week containing a Monday bank holiday uses **4 days** from the 25-day annual-leave balance, not 5. The bank holiday remains separate. If the employee does not normally work Mondays, People Operations may adjust the part-time calculation so that their overall paid-holiday entitlement remains proportionate rather than being driven by which weekday a public holiday happens to fall on.

### Carry-over example

An employee finishes 2026 with 7 unused annual-leave days. Their manager approves the standard maximum **5-day carry-over**. Those 5 days should normally be taken by 31 March 2027. The remaining 2 days do not automatically carry unless a statutory protection or exceptional written HR decision applies.

### Sickness during booked holiday

An employee becomes medically unfit for work for three days during a booked week of annual leave. They contact their manager and People Operations promptly and provide reasonable evidence when asked. People Operations may reclassify the qualifying days as sickness absence and restore those days to the annual-leave balance. The employee may not simply edit the record months later without review.

### Family-leave pay example

An eligible UK employee starts maternity leave and receives Northline's enhanced package. The first 16 weeks are paid at 100% of normal base salary inclusive of statutory maternity pay. Northline does not pay 100% salary **plus** statutory maternity pay as a second payment; the statutory amount is included within the company top-up. The same inclusive approach applies to the other enhanced statutory-pay arrangements in this policy.

## Pregnancy and adoption appointments

A pregnant UK employee may take paid time off for qualifying antenatal care in line with statutory rights in force at the time. Managers should not require an employee to use annual leave for a qualifying statutory appointment. People Operations may ask for appointment evidence after the first appointment where permitted.

For adoption, surrogacy and partner appointments, statutory rights differ by role and circumstance. The company-paid partner appointment benefit described above is intended to cover ordinary cases without requiring managers to interpret detailed eligibility rules. Where a statutory entitlement is more favourable, the statutory entitlement prevails.

## Annual leave before and after family leave

Employees are encouraged to discuss accrued annual leave with People Operations before a long period of family leave. A common arrangement is to take some annual leave immediately before leave starts or immediately after it ends.

Managers should not insist that all accrued leave be taken in one block if that would be unreasonable, but the team may need a plan where a long family-leave period crosses the 31 December holiday-year end. Statutory carry-over protections are handled by People Operations rather than through the standard 5-day carry-over cap.

## Keeping-in-touch and work during leave

People Operations will explain any lawful keeping-in-touch or shared-parental-in-touch arrangements that apply to UK statutory leave. Participation is voluntary where the law makes it voluntary, and a manager should not use a keeping-in-touch day to create an informal expectation that the employee continues ordinary duties while on leave.

For company-paid parental leave in Austin, an employee on full leave is not expected to monitor email or remain routinely available. A genuinely optional social or planning contact does not convert leave into ordinary working time.

## Returning from family leave

Before an expected return, the manager and employee should discuss the planned date, any accrued annual leave, system access that needs reactivation and whether a phased or flexible arrangement has been requested.

Where an employee asks to change working hours or location after family leave, the request should be considered under the applicable flexible-working process and local law. It should not be rejected simply because the employee took family leave.

Managers should avoid scheduling a major performance process solely because an employee has just returned from protected leave. Legitimate pre-existing performance matters may continue, but People Operations should review timing and evidence.

## Abuse, inaccurate records and overpayments

Northline may investigate deliberate falsification of leave or sickness records under `NL-HR-CODE-2026`. Genuine mistakes should normally be corrected administratively.

Where Payroll overpays an enhanced leave benefit because of an administrative error, Finance and People Operations will explain the correction and, where repayment is appropriate, agree a reasonable recovery method. No manager should deduct money informally from expenses or another payment.

## Interaction with performance, bonus and commission

Annual leave and ordinary sickness should not be treated as misconduct. Longer absences may affect variable-pay calculations where the applicable plan lawfully uses active-service or revenue criteria. The plan document and local law control; managers should not invent their own pro-rating rule.

Sales commission already earned under the relevant plan is handled under that plan. Enhanced parental pay in this policy refers to base salary unless a written compensation plan states otherwise.

## Record corrections

Employees should review leave balances periodically. A suspected error should be raised within 30 days of discovery where practical. People Operations can correct a balance after checking manager approvals, payroll records and statutory leave periods.

An employee leaving Northline will have unused or overused annual leave handled under their contract, local law and final-pay process. Managers should not promise a particular deduction or payout before People Operations and Payroll confirm it.

## Ownership and review

Policy owner: Head of People. Operational administrator: People Operations. Payroll is responsible for implementing approved pay instructions. This policy is reviewed annually or earlier if a material legal or company-benefit change occurs.

This synthetic policy uses official UK guidance as its statutory background but is intentionally written as an internal operating policy rather than a legal summary.
